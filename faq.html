Frequently Asked Questions About Customized Water Bottles

Answers to common questions about BRANDSIP customized water bottles, sizes, shapes, labels, pricing and customization. For information not covered here, contact BRANDSIP for current availability and requirements.

Customized water bottles are packaged drinking water bottles where the label is customized with a restaurant name, company name, business name, brand name, logo, event name or artwork. BRANDSIP supplies bottled water with customized labels for businesses, restaurants, hotels, events and organizations.

A branded water bottle is a bottled water bottle that carries a brand's name, logo or design on the label. BRANDSIP supplies branded packaged drinking water so a business does not have to serve generic-looking water bottles.
