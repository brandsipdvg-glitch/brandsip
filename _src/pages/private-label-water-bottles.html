Private Label

Bottle Packaged Drinking Water Under Your Own Brand

Private label water (also called private label drinking water) is packaged drinking water produced and bottled under your brand instead of a national label. You decide the name, the logo, the colours and the look. BRANDSIP handles treatment, bottling, labelling and delivery — so the product on the table is unmistakably yours.

  • ✓ You own the brand — no competitor logo competes for attention on the bottle
  • ✓ Consistent identity across restaurants, hotels, retail shelves and events
  • ✓ A premium private label bottle creates a more established, polished impression
  • ✓ Flexible MOQs and negotiable pricing for single outlets, chains and new launches
