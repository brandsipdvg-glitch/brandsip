Water Bottle Sizes Explained: 250ml, 500ml, 750ml and 1 Litre

Choosing the right water bottle size is essential for any branded water order. This guide explains the differences between 250ml, 500ml, 750ml and 1 litre bottles, and helps you decide which size fits your restaurant, hotel, event or business best.

Product

Read time: ~8 minutes · BRANDSIP Guide

1. 250ml Bottles

Compact single-serve bottles are ideal for sampling, takeaways and short events where a small bottle fits best. They're easy to distribute and match occasions where a full serving isn't needed.

2. 500ml Bottles

The most popular choice, 500ml bottles suit restaurant tables, hotel rooms, conferences and most events. They balance a comfortable single-serve with a generous branding area.

3. 750ml Bottles

A midsize option between a single serve and a large format, 750ml bottles work well for longer meetings, hospitality suites and gatherings where a bit more water is appreciated.

4. 1 Litre Bottles

The largest option offers the biggest branding canvas and suits long stays, restaurant tables, offices and large events. The 1 litre round bottle is BRANDSIP's premium large-format choice.

5. Matching Size to Occasion

Consider the setting, duration and guest count. Many businesses combine sizes — small bottles for quick service, larger bottles for extended hospitality.
