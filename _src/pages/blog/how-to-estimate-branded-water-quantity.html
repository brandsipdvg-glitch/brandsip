How to Estimate the Right Quantity of Branded Water for Your Event

Ordering too little or too much branded water wastes money and opportunity. This guide helps you estimate the right quantity of branded water for your event, based on attendance, duration and type of occasion.

Buying Guide

Read time: ~8 minutes · BRANDSIP Guide

1. Start with Your Attendee Count

The clearest starting point is your number of guests or attendees. For a wedding, consider the number of functions and whether guests carry over between events.

2. Account for Event Duration

The longer the event, the more water each person may need. A short product launch may need one bottle per person, while a full-day conference may need two.

3. Consider the Setting

Indoor, climate-controlled settings may need less water than outdoor or warm venues. Serving style also matters — seated dining, buffets and takeaway all differ.

4. Add a Comfortable Buffer

It's wise to order a small buffer to account for unexpected demand, extra guests or spillage. A 5–10% buffer is a common safety margin.

5. When in Doubt, Ask for Help

Estimating is easier with guidance. Share your guest count and event plan with BRANDSIP, and we'll recommend a sensible quantity.

6. Finalize with Confirmation

Once you've confirmed your quantity, lock in your order details early so production and delivery are scheduled comfortably before the event.
