Branded Water for Events vs Weddings: What’s Different?

Branded water serves different purposes at corporate events and weddings. This article compares the two, covering how design, sizing and personalization differ — so you can plan branded water that fits your specific occasion.

Events

Read time: ~8 minutes · BRANDSIP Guide

1. Different Audiences, Different Goals

Corporate events aim to reinforce a company's professional identity, while weddings create personal, celebratory memories. Your branded water should reflect these different goals.

2. Design and Personalization

Corporate bottles lean toward clean, professional branding with company logos. Wedding bottles often feature names, dates and theme colours for a personal, keepsake feel.

3. Sizing Considerations

Both events and weddings commonly use 500ml bottles, but durations and guest counts vary. Longer events or receptions may benefit from 1 litre bottles.

4. Quantities and Planning

Corporate event quantities are based on attendance, while weddings consider guest count across multiple functions. Plan accordingly to avoid shortages.

5. Common Ground: Premium Presentation

Regardless of occasion, a clean, well-produced bottle elevates the experience. Consistent, quality branding works for both weddings and events.
