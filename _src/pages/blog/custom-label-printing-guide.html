Custom Label Printing for Water Bottles: A Practical Guide

The label is the face of your branded water bottle. This practical guide covers custom label printing for water bottles — from single-side stickers to full-cover wraps — so you can prepare artwork that looks great and prints reliably.

Design

Read time: ~8 minutes · BRANDSIP Guide

1. Single-Side vs Full-Cover Labels

Single-side labels are simple and cost-effective, ideal for square bottles. Full-cover labels wrap the entire bottle, offering a larger branding canvas and a more premium finish on round bottles.

2. Preparing Your Artwork

Provide high-resolution logos and clean files. Clear, legible design with adequate contrast prints reliably and looks professional at a glance.

3. Choosing the Right Finish

Your choice of finish affects the premium feel and durability of the label. Discuss your desired look with BRANDSIP to select the best option for your bottle.

4. Typography and Text Placement

Ensure your brand name, logo and any mandatory text (like MRP or product information) are placed clearly and legibly, respecting the label dimensions.

5. Checking Before Production

Review a proof or sample before bulk production. Confirming accuracy at this stage prevents costly errors and ensures your brand is represented exactly as intended.

6. Ordering with BRANDSIP

Share your branding and BRANDSIP will prepare and produce the label that best matches your bottle and budget.
