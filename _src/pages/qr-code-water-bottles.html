QR Marketing

Make Every Bottle a Marketing Channel

QR code water bottles are FSSAI-compliant packaged drinking water bottles with a scannable QR code printed on the label at production. Unlike generic bottles that carry no message, a QR-enabled branded bottle does three jobs at once: it carries your logo, it improves your presentation, and it opens a direct digital connection with every customer who picks it up.
