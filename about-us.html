About BRANDSIP

BRANDSIP is a customized packaged drinking water business focused on helping businesses, hospitality establishments, organizations and events create a branded experience through customized water bottles.

Our Story

Brandsip Was Born from a Simple Observation

Businesses invest significant time, effort, and money building their brand identity. They carefully design their spaces, create memorable customer experiences, and work hard to earn customer trust. Yet, when they serve drinking water, they often use bottles carrying another company's label.

Every bottle placed on a restaurant table, hotel room desk, office reception, event venue, or customer meeting becomes an opportunity to reinforce a brand. Instead, that opportunity is often given away to someone else's brand.

This realization sparked the idea behind Brandsip.

We set out to help businesses transform an everyday necessity into a powerful branding tool. By providing high-quality packaged drinking water bottles customized with each client's brand, we enable businesses to strengthen their brand presence with every bottle they serve.

What was once a simple water bottle becomes a lasting brand touchpoint—one that customers see, hold, and remember.

Our Vision

Our Vision

To make every water bottle a branding asset and help businesses build stronger brand recall through everyday customer experiences.

Our Mission

Our Mission

To provide businesses with high-quality packaged drinking water bottles customized with their own branding, enabling them to enhance brand visibility, create a more professional customer experience, and maximize every interaction with their customers.
