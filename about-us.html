About BRANDSIP

BRANDSIP is a customized packaged drinking water business focused on helping businesses, hospitality establishments, organizations and events create a branded experience through customized water bottles.

What We Do

Packaged Drinking Water + Custom Branding

BRANDSIP supplies packaged drinking water in multiple bottle sizes and shapes. Customers add their restaurant name, company name, business name, brand name, logo, event name or artwork to the bottle label — turning a functional product into part of their brand presentation.

Every BRANDSIP bottle is filtered, UV treated and ozonised before packaging.

Why Customized Water Bottles

A business does not have to serve generic-looking water bottles. A branded bottle carries your brand identity and becomes part of the customer experience.

Bottles to Choose From

BRANDSIP offers 300ml, 500ml and 1 litre bottle options with square and round formats. Round bottles can use a complete-wrap, full-cover label for a premium appearance.

Focus on Customization

BRANDSIP focuses on customization and presentation, helping businesses, hospitality establishments, organizations and events look polished and professional.
