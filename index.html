Introduction

What Are Customised Water Bottles?

When a guest pours a glass of water in your restaurant, picks up a bottle from the hotel mini-bar, or reaches for one at your wedding, do they remember who offered it to them? With customised water bottles from BRANDSIP, the answer is yes — because the bottle itself carries your brand.

Customised water bottles are bottled drinking water printed with your logo, business name, event monogram, artwork or a scannable QR code. Unlike generic bottles that look the same on every table, a branded bottle turns a daily necessity into a brand asset guests hold, photograph and often keep.

BRANDSIP makes this easy, supplying custom branded, private label and QR code water bottles for hotels, restaurants, cafes, resorts, corporate offices, weddings and events — with FSSAI-compliant packaged drinking water, custom label design, bulk orders and fast delivery behind every bottle.
